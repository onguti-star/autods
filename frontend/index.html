▶ ◇ What-if simulation — estimate numeric scenarios

Ask: “If this driver changed, what would probably happen to this outcome?” AutoDS resamples your rows many times, applies the scenario, and reports the likely range of answers.

1. Outcome
The number you care about, such as revenue, profit, cost, units sold, GDP, or population.
2. Driver
The number you want to change. If it is not the outcome, AutoDS estimates its linear relationship to the outcome.
3. Trials
Repeated what-if runs. More trials make the range steadier, but take a little longer.

Train on targets such as fraud, risk, default, approved, or status.
Revenue outcomes
Predict numeric targets like sales, revenue, profit, price, amount, or income.
Dimensionality Reduction (PCA)

Run PCA to compress numeric features before training. This can speed up training and reduce overfitting when columns are highly correlated.

06 / Unsupervised Learning

Discover patterns without a target column — clustering, anomaly detection, dimensionality reduction, and association rules.
